ℹ️ IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

ALARM ERATH is an informational monitor, not a certified early-warning or life-safety system. USGS reports detected and processed earthquakes; this dashboard cannot predict earthquakes or guarantee advance warning. Data may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. Do not wait for this website or a notification before taking protective action. Follow local emergency-management instructions. During shaking, Drop, Cover, and Hold On. This app does not contact emergency services.
